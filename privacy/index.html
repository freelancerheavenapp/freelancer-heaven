Privacy Policy

Freelancer Time Tracker
Last updated: October 28, 2026

Your data

Freelancer Time Tracker stores your projects, hourly rates, timer records, earnings estimates, language and currency preferences locally on your device.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use your personal data for advertising, analytics, or tracking. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Backup and exports

You may choose to create encrypted local backups or export reports. These files are created on your device and remain under your control. Backup files are protected with the PIN you choose.

Permissions

The app may request access needed to save or restore files only when you choose to export a report or import a backup.

Children

This app is intended for adult freelancers and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version will always be available on this page.
